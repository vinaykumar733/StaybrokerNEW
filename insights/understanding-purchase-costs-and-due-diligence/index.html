Understanding purchase costs and due diligence

In India, the total cost of a property decision often extends beyond the headline price. Buyers and investors should understand the full picture before committing to a transaction so they can avoid surprises later in the process.

Look beyond the purchase price

Additional costs may include registration, stamp duties, legal review, and other property-related expenses that vary by context. A sound decision usually begins with a realistic assessment of the total financial outlay.

Review the paperwork carefully

Documentation matters. Buyers should be comfortable reviewing the available records, required approvals, and any transaction-stage questions that could affect confidence in the deal.

Use due diligence as a risk filter

Practical due diligence helps clients check that the opportunity aligns with their needs and that there are no obvious red flags before they commit time, energy, and capital.

Consult before signing

When a buyer is uncertain, speaking with a property advisor before concluding a transaction can help clarify the key issues without rushing a decision.

Checklist

  • Cost breakdown review
  • Legal and document review
  • Risk and suitability assessment
